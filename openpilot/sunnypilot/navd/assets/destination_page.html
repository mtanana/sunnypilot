    Map
    Mapbox is the map service that the sunnypilot navigation engine uses to search for routes and fetch the map tiles to render. To do this, a Mapbox public key with only the styles:tiles scope is required. Every request will be billed to this public key, but it supports up to 100,000 route requests or 200,000 map tile requests a month before it begins billing at $2.00 and $0.50 per 1,000, respectively (pricing).
    Away from home wifi the device needs a data connection (comma prime/eSIM or mobile hotspot) to search for a new route.
    A token is needed in two places: your phone and your comma. The simple setup is to use a token that can request map tiles for both, even though the comma does not need this permission. Mapbox's token management best practices page recommends one key per use case, restricted to only the features it needs, so you can optionally set a second, more restricted token for the comma below. This page keeps its phone token in this phone's browser. The sunnynav app keeps its own.

          Settings

          Allow Navigation (required)
          The navigation service that calculates routes on the comma. Away from home wifi it needs a data connection (comma prime/eSIM or mobile hotspot) to route.
          Use Route Turn Desires
          When you signal for a turn on your route, the car plans that turn at the intersection instead of guessing from the road ahead.
          Slow for Route Turns
          Slow down ahead of each turn on your route to the speed drivers take a corner of that angle. Applies to turns of 55 degrees or more.
          Guidance on Screen
          Show Lanes
          Show which lanes lead to the next maneuver.
          Route Lane Change Delay
          A separate lane change delay for changes requested by the navigation logic.
          Turn Sounds
          A tone pair for each maneuver: rising means right, falling means left, faster and doubled when the turn is close.
          Always Show Next Turn
          Off, the arrow for the next turn appears as the turn approaches and clears after it. On, a dim arrow for the next turn stays in the corner the whole route.
          Speed Limit Sign
          The posted limit of the road ahead, from the route, as a sign on this page, the phone app and the head unit map.
          Mapbox Token (optional)
          Draws the map behind the route line. Must be a public token (starts with pk.). Follow these instructions to create a token, then copy it from your account dashboard and paste it here. It is kept in this browser and never leaves your phone.
          Device Token (required)
          The device's own public token, for its route requests and search. It needs no scopes at all, so a token restricted to nothing more is the safest choice. The phone's token works here too. Away from home wifi the device needs a data connection (comma prime/eSIM or mobile hotspot) to use it. It stays on the comma and is not saved in your browser.
          Reroute Automatically (optional)
          Recompute the route automatically when you leave it. The device needs a data connection (comma prime/eSIM or mobile hotspot) for this to work, otherwise the route, and navigation, is lost.
